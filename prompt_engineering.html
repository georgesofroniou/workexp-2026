01 — A quick introduction

Prompting is not about sounding clever. It is about being clear.

AI does not read your mind. It responds to the instructions you give it. The better the instruction, the more useful the answer is likely to be.

02 — AI fundamentals

Generative AI is only one part of the wider AI puzzle

Think of AI as the umbrella term. Under it you have rules-based systems, machine learning, generative AI and large language models.

Artificial IntelligenceSoftware that seems to make smart choices. Example: a social feed ranking posts.
Rules-based AIFixed if/then logic for predictable results. Example: a support bot using menu options.
Machine LearningLearns patterns from data over time. Example: TikTok learning what you rewatch.
Generative AI + LLMsCreates new text, images, summaries, slides or code. LLMs are especially focused on language.

03 — The big rule

Vague prompt = vague answer.
Clear prompt = useful answer.

If the answer is weak, upgrade the instructions before blaming the tool.

weak: Explain climate change.
better: Explain climate change to a 16-year-old GCSE Geography student in 200 words.

AI is smart, but it is not psychic.

04 — Use this checklist

The GCSCE prompt framework

Goal. Context. Specifics. Constraints. Evaluate. A simple structure students can remember and reuse.

G

Goal

What am I asking for?
C

Context

What does AI need to know?
S

Specifics

What should be included?
C

Constraints

How long, tone, format?
E

Evaluate

How can it improve or check?

05 — Confidence ladder

Three prompting levels

01

Basic prompting

Ask clearly. Example: “Explain photosynthesis in simple terms.” Good for quick explanations and first drafts, but AI still has to guess your level, format and detail.

02

Intermediate prompting

Add context and format. Example: “Create a GCSE revision summary about photosynthesis for a 16-year-old Biology student. Use simple bullet points and finish with one quiz question.”

03

Advanced prompting

Add a role and process. Example: “Act as a friendly GCSE Biology tutor. Explain simply, give an analogy, quiz me, correct mistakes and give one revision tip.”

06 — Prompt choice

Different prompts for different jobs

There is no single perfect prompt. Match the prompt to the task.

Study

Ask for revision help, explanations and practice questions.

Images

Describe what the camera should see.

Slides

Give the AI the brief, not just the topic.

Creative

Set the style, voice and limits.

Career

Ask for advice matched to your experience.
